# Business Model: Metal Working Machine Tool Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7223`
- ISCO-08: `7223`
- Occupation: Metal Working Machine Tool Setters and Operators
- Social impact: worker-safety, precision-quality, tooling-continuity

## Customer

- CNC / manual machine shops (lathe, mill, press)
- independent machining crews / crew cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- task/batch/materials-usage/progress-record logging
- tooling-materials supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to shop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a machine-setup or machining-execution
  decision (e.g. a specific lathe, mill or press setup/run), ever
- no override of a shop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (rotating-machinery hazard, tooling-change
  injury risk, metal-shaving hazard) always route to human sign-off,
  regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
